Diffusion models, intuition first

How a model learns to make new images by learning to remove noise, and how ten years of papers made that fast, steerable and cheap.

Above, computed for a one-dimensional toy: three clusters of data, Improved DDPM's cosine noise schedule, and the exact best denoiser in place of a trained network. Forward paths add random noise; reverse paths follow the deterministic DDIM sampler.
