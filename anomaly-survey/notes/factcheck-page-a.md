## Problems not fixed, and why

- Setup values are bare numbers, but no claim holds them as a value: MVTec AD 2 Fig. 4 regions "4, 2 and 20 pixels", the VisA toy "100 anomalous samples" and "10 false alarms", and the MVTec AD 2 Sec. 4.2.1 example "10-pixel defect", "5-megapixel" and "5%". Each was checked against the source (figures/auc_pro_vs_roc.pdf, spot-diff.tex App. B, MAD2_main.tex l.625). These are not results, and the claims files are outside this task.
- Our-computation numbers stay as labeled prose: SPADE gallery 156,800, PaDiM 1792, 303,050, 3,136 and 3.80 GB. Claim ids exist (spade-gallery-size, padim-wr50-dim, padim-store-*, padim-positions), but they are marked uncertain. check-page fails when an uncertain claim is shown as fact, so the wraps were reverted.
- ch-padim says "Our computation explains the number (3.8 GB)". The 3.80 GB covers the Gaussians only. Sec. V-D says PaDiM also keeps the CNN in memory, so the match with Table VIII is approximate. Left as is, because the paper does not itemize Table VIII.
- ch-padim says "about 240 training images". PaDiM writes "15 classes of approximately 240 images" and does not say "training". 3,629 / 15 = 242 supports the training reading, so no change.
- notes/understanding.md l.35 and l.239 say PatchCore layers are "pooled, not concatenated". That conflicts with fix 14. Not edited, because it is outside this task.
