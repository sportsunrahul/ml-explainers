Six families on one map

The field split into families, and each family answers "what is the model of normal?" in its own way. They did not replace each other: they grew side by side, and the newest growth is in one model for many products and in vision-language models.

Predict before you press: which family started latest, and which grew fastest after it started? Press "Vision-language". It has no papers before 2023 and then becomes the largest family. "Multi-class" follows the same curve one step behind. "Normalizing flow" stays small throughout. "Memory bank" grows slowly and never fades: the oldest idea on the page is still in use.

How the counts were made

Four paginated arXiv searches (abstracts naming MVTec, abstracts naming "industrial anomaly", titles with "anomaly detection" whose abstracts mention defects, manufacturing or industry, and abstracts naming VisA) returned 1,165 unique papers. Keeping only image papers that mention a benchmark, industry, defects, surfaces or inspection, and dropping surveillance-video work, leaves 780. The subset grew from 18 papers in 2020 to 101 in 2023 and 201 in 2025, and 2026 had 191 by early October.

Over the whole subset, the keyword matches rank like this: reconstruction 203, vision-language 202, synthetic defects 153, multi-class 142, memory bank 80, student-teacher 52, normalizing flow 19. Edge and real-time words appear in 98. Read these as a shape, not a census: arXiv search misses papers that use none of the terms, and keyword matching misfiles some papers. A paper that says "reconstruction" in passing counts as reconstruction.

The page follows these lanes. Act II stays in the first family, where the model of normal is a set of stored features, and refines it three times. Act III tries two other answers: learn the density of normal features exactly, or invent defects and train a classifier. Act IV makes the detector fast and then makes one detector serve many products. Act V removes the training images altogether. Act VI puts the published timings of many of these methods on one chart of speed against accuracy.
