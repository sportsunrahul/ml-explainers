Inside LLaVA: forward, then backward

The connector figure showed how many tokens a picture costs; here is the whole training step around it, with the tensor shapes at every stage. The variant buttons switch between the two papers and between their two training stages, which differ in exactly one thing: which boxes are allowed to learn.

The step counts show how small this recipe is. LLaVA's stage 1 is about 4,650 updates and its stage 2 about 14,800; LLaVA-1.5's are about 2,180 and about 5,200, larger batches over more data in a single epoch. Stage 1 is cheap for another reason too: it updates only the projector, a single 1024 \times d_{\text{LLM}} matrix in LLaVA, though every one of its steps still pays for a full forward and backward pass through the frozen language model.
