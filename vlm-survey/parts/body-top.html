Almost every model on this page has the same three parts: a vision encoder that turns pixels into vectors, a connector that hands those vectors over, and a language model that reads them and writes. One color key holds for the whole page:

vision: pixels, patches, image embeddings language: text tokens, captions, the LLM the bridge, a correct match loss, error, cost frozen weights, negatives, background

Each interactive carries a badge. Computed means a real formula from a paper, evaluated directly or run on a small toy. Toy model means an illustration of the mechanism, not the paper's numbers. Sourced means what is drawn comes from the papers themselves: numbers from their tables, or structure and dates as they state them (the family tree's arcs are the page's own reading). In an equation, hover or tap a term that names part of a figure: that part of the figure lights up.
