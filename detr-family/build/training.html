One training step, end to end

This chapter has no narration: step through it at your own pace.

You have now seen every part of DETR. Here they are running together in one training step: a batch leaves the data loader, flows through the network, gets matched to its ground truth, and becomes one loss and one weight update. Click a stage or press Next to walk through it, or Run a step to watch the whole pass. New batch draws fresh augmented image sizes, and every tensor shape below updates with them.

At inference there is no matching and no loss: take the last decoder layer's 100 slots and give each one its most likely real class (ignoring "no object") and its box. For its COCO AP numbers the paper keeps all of them this way, even slots whose top class is "no object", and reports that this scores 2 AP higher than filtering those slots out. To draw a clean picture you would instead keep only the slots whose score clears a threshold. No NMS either way.

Training repeats this step for every batch: 300 epochs with the learning rate cut tenfold after 200 for the paper's ablations, or 500 epochs with the drop after 400 for the headline model. The paper reports 3 days on 16 V100 GPUs for the shorter schedule. Why it needs so many epochs is where the next chapter starts.
