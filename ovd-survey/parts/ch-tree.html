Family tree

Six papers carried this page, and a family grew around them. Here are 22 of them on one time axis: each sits at the date of its first arXiv version, in a lane for where text enters the network.

Guess first. Between 2021 and 2025, did text move deeper into the detector, or back out of it? Press Spine only, then Add the branches, and read each lane from left to right.

It did both. From 2021 to 2023 the grounding line pushed text deep into the network: MDETR (2021-04-26) reads image and text tokens in one joint encoder, GLIP (2021-12-07) fuses them in its top encoder layers, and Grounding DINO (2023-03-09) adds text at three places. Then the cost of fusion pulled text back out. YOLO-World (2024-01-30) folds its neck fusion into ordinary weights, OV-DINO (2024-07-10) keeps Grounding DINO's query selection but takes text out of the encoder (the hollow dot), and YOLOE (2025-03-10) removes YOLO-World's neck fusion and keeps text at the classifier only our reading.

The classifier-only lane never went away. It runs from OVR-CNN (2020-11-20), which named the task, through ViLD and Detic to OWL-ViT and OWLv2. These models pay the least for a new prompt, because the text encoder runs once per class name, whatever the image. The grey squares are not detectors: they are the tests that chapters 11 to 13 rely on, and all four came out after Grounding DINO.
