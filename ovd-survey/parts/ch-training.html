Interlude: one training step (not narrated)

One Grounding DINO training step, end to end

Chapter 9 introduced each part of Grounding DINO. Here they run together in one training step on one GPU: a batch of images and prompts goes through both backbones, the enhancer, query selection and the decoder, gets matched to its ground truth, and becomes one loss and one weight update. Click a stage or press Next, or Run a step to watch the whole pass. New batch draws new images and prompts, and every tensor shape in the readout updates with them.

The paper stops there: it gives no pre-training epochs and no learning-rate schedule. MM-Grounding-DINO's re-implementation trains for 30 epochs at a total batch of 128 on 32 NVIDIA 3090 GPUs, but that is its own schedule, not the original one. At inference there is no matching and no loss. Keep the last layer's queries, read each one's box and token logits, and keep the boxes whose phrase scores clear a threshold that you choose.
