How they fail on rare industrial objects

Seven causes, three you can see here

The name means something else on the web

A text encoder places "porosity" where the web uses it: rock, skin, foam. The weld pores sit elsewhere. A plain description, "small round holes in a weld", lands near them. GLIP measured the effect: on one dataset, describing the look raised zero-shot AP from 13.6 to 73.8.

The sphere is a toy with hand-placed points; the GLIP numbers are from its Table 10.

Small defects vanish at the input size

OWL-ViT B/32 resizes your image to 768 pixels and reads 32-pixel patches. A 20-pixel defect in a 4096-pixel frame becomes under 4 pixels: a small fraction of one patch. Crop or tile instead.

Computed from the model's input size, assuming a 4:3 image scaled by its long side (our reading of the preprocessing).

A threshold trades false alarms for misses

With no score threshold, Grounding DINO puts a box on every image, including those without the target. A 0.9 threshold cuts those false alarms to 8.5%, and mAP falls from 20.7 to 3.8.
