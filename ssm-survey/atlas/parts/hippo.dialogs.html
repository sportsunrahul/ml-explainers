Legendre polynomial

A family of polynomials P_0, P_1, P_2, ... on the interval from -1 to 1: P_0 = 1, P_1 = x, P_2 = (3x² - 1)/2, and so on. Any two different ones are orthogonal: the integral of their product is zero. That makes them a clean basis: the best fit of degree below N is found one coefficient at a time, and adding a degree never changes the coefficients you already have.

HiPPO-LegS stretches them over the history [0, t] and scales P_n by the square root of 2n + 1 so that each has unit size.

Online function approximation

Approximating a signal while it is still arriving. At each moment you have seen the signal up to now, and you must keep a fixed-size summary that lets you rebuild it as well as possible. "Online" means you update the summary from the new value only, without going back over the past.

HiPPO's summary is the N coefficients of the best polynomial fit, and it shows that updating them is a linear equation.
