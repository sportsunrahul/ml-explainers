S4: one system, three views

Fixed weights turn the recurrence into one long convolution

Drop HiPPO's time factor and keep its matrix A: the now uses the same A, B and C at every step. Then it can be computed three ways. Continuous: a differential equation. Recurrent: update the state once per token, like an RNN. Convolutional: slide one long over the input.

The dots (recurrent) and rings (convolution) land on exactly the same numbers. The green curve (continuous) agrees up to the small error of turning the equation into steps. Training uses the convolution, all positions at once with an ; generation uses the recurrence, one cheap step per token.

The yellow row is the kernel: entry j is how much the input j steps back adds to the output now. Drag the step size: a smaller Δ stretches the same memory over more tokens.

Building this kernel naively costs N²L operations, and HiPPO's A cannot be diagonalized safely. S4 writes A as normal plus , works in frequency space where all the powers of A become one inverse, and gets the kernel in about N + L operations. It was the first model above chance on .
