Convolution kernel

A list of weights K_0, K_1, K_2, ... applied to a sequence by sliding: each output is K_0 times the current input, plus K_1 times the input one step back, plus K_2 times the input two steps back, and so on. A time-invariant SSM is exactly such a convolution, with K_j = C Ā^j B̄; its kernel is as long as the sequence.

FFT (fast Fourier transform)

An algorithm that turns a sequence of length L into its frequencies in about L log L steps instead of L². A convolution becomes a simple product in frequency space, so a long convolution costs three FFTs and one multiplication, not L² multiply-adds.

Low rank

A matrix of rank r can be written as a sum of r outer products, each a column vector times a row vector. A rank-1 matrix is one such product, P Q^T, and stores only 2N numbers instead of N². S4 writes HiPPO's A as a matrix that a stable change of basis makes diagonal, plus a rank-1 correction, and handles the correction with the Woodbury formula.

Long Range Arena (LRA) and Path-X

A benchmark of six classification tasks on long inputs, from 1K to 16K tokens: nested list operations, text, document retrieval, images read pixel by pixel, and two path-finding tasks. In Path-X a 128 x 128 image is read as one sequence of 16,384 pixels, and the model must say whether two dots are joined by a path. Before S4 every model scored at chance, 50%.
