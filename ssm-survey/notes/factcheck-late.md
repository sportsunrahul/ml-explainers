Method: every claim in notes/claims/{recall,hybrid,branches}.json and every notes/claims/page*.json present at the final pass was opened at its loc in the primary source
(tex, PDF text or rendered figure, abs.html, README) and checked for verbatim value, row/column/model/budget, version and flags.
All arithmetic was redone. arXiv dates and versions were checked by script against each abs.html submission history.
The 9 mamba.json ids that the page uses were also checked. No claim file was edited.

Result: no numeric value is wrong. The problems are wording or scope (the value is right but the claim says more than the source),
missing uncertain/our_reading flags where the paper contradicts itself, and wrong locations.
