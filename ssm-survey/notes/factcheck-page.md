## Checked and found correct (selection)

- ch-bill/atlas/interlude arithmetic: 128 KiB per token (Llama 3 8B), 12,451,840 B = 11.875 MiB (Mamba 2.8B, N=16 plus 3 conv inputs), 95 tokens, 15 GiB weights, crossover near 122,000 tokens, 1/16/128 GiB at 8K/128K/1M; 81,920 + 15,360 numbers per layer; 39.3M and 218M weights per layer; 2,048 KV numbers per layer.
- 5x throughput wording ("each at the largest batch it fits; smaller at equal batch") matches the claim note (about 4x at batch 32); 1814 / 364 = 5.0.
- Mamba Table 1 (selective copying: Hyena listed under the H3 architecture is correct) and Table 11 (induction heads, MHA OOM beyond 2^14 = 16384, Hyena "perfect" at 2^9).
- HiPPO Table 2: best baselines per shift, ">= 95% without shift", "fall by 25 points or more" all hold. HiPPO computed figure recomputed independently in numpy: RMSE 0.750 / 0.234 / 0.0133 (N = 4/16/64), 0.0118 at t=20 (N=16), Euler 0.296 and peak 1.0e44. The test function is App F.8's.
- Recurrence toy: 0.9^50 = 0.52%, 0.01^(1/16384) = 0.99972, gains 5.3/50/500; scan toy: 22 combines and 7 rounds at 8 tokens, 9 rounds at 16, 23 at 2,048.
- LSSL Lemma 3.1 (backward Euler, Delta = e^z), HiPPO Sec 2.5 on gates, S4 Lemma 3.2 (2^{4N/3}), S4 Tables 3/10/11 numbering, Martin and Cundy O(6n(T/p + lg p)) vs O(2nT), Mamba-2 Theorem 5.2 wording, Mamba-2 "N around 64 or 128" (architecture.tex:74), MQAR plot readings (0.95 vs 0.50 at length 256; about 0.52 vs about 0 at 1024), Mamba audio limitation, VMamba ERF vs Vim (atlas), Mamba-3 intro on Mamba-2 and GDN, H3 two-layer synthetics, Illusion "Mamba needs no more layers than the transformer" (src :733).
- Hybrid KV markers: 4 KiB per attention layer per token, Llama-2 7B 512 KiB (4x the all-attention line), Jamba-1.5-Large 36 KiB = 9 GiB at 256K, Nemotron-H 56B 10 x 4 KiB.
- ch-map monthly range (48 to 112 since mid-2024) supports "about 50 and 110"; 12 read + 28 tree papers = 40 nodes.
- Badges: every data-toy figure carries "Toy model"; Computed figures (bill, hippo, s4 views, s4/mamba/mamba2/vim/jamba flows, scan memory, interlude, ssd, hybrid) run paper formulas or configs, with our-reading parts labelled; Sourced figures are the arXiv scan and the family tree.
