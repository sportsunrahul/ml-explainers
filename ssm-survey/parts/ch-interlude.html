Interlude: one generated token, end to end

One token through two models

Every chapter so far looked at one piece. Here is the whole path of a single generated token, through a Mamba-2.8B-sized model running its recurrence beside a Llama-3-8B-sized Transformer reading its KV cache. Press through the stages and change the context length: watch which memory changes size.

The two columns differ in exactly one box per layer, and that box is the subject of this page: a memory of fixed size that is updated, against one that keeps everything and is reread. The next chapter shows the two are closer relatives than they look.
