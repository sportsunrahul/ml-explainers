The 2026 explosion

How big is this field? On 2026-10-08 the arXiv API returned 569 unique papers whose title contains JEPA or whose abstract names a joint-embedding predictive architecture: 7 in 2023, 38 in 2024, 76 in 2025, and 447 so far in 2026 (416 in its first nine months, 97 of them in September alone; one earlier paper is from 2022). The curve is the shape of a field being discovered.

Claim card: how this count was made, and what it misses

Claim. At least 569 arXiv papers name JEPA in their title or abstract, and the monthly rate in 2026 is several times that of 2025.

How. The arXiv API, paginated to completion; the raw records with abstracts are kept in the project. What it misses: papers that use the idea without the name (DINO-WM, Navigation World Models and PLDM all needed to be added by hand), non-arXiv venues, and the title-keyword domain split cannot see a paper's method. What would change it: a different query, or a classifier run on the abstracts rather than the titles.

What the field is arguing about

Read the 2026 titles and five questions recur, each with a paper that names it.

  • One future or many? A deterministic predictor returns one latent successor "even when hidden intent, partial observation, or stochastic dynamics make several futures plausible" (Branch-JEPA, 2026-07-06; Var-JEPA and a variational JEPA, 2026-01-20, give the probabilistic answer).
  • Does a single frame know its velocity? "A single-frame embedding carries no velocity information, for any encoder, including the official released LeWM weights" (Frozen in a Frame, 2026-10-03): a world model that encodes one frame at a time cannot represent momentum.
  • Is forward prediction enough? Forward prediction "does not explicitly enforce reliable identifiability of robot-centric physical state" (Physical State Grounding, 2026-08-07); H-JEPA's inverse-dynamics fix on DROID is the same concern.
  • Is the latent distance the right cost? Training "optimizes short-horizon latent prediction, whereas planning requires a multi-step ranking of imagined futures by goal progress" (Temporal-Distance JEPA, 2026-07-28).
  • When does training collapse? A linearised gradient flow gives a per-mode stability ratio and a predicted phase boundary (Drive vs. Decay, 2026-10-01), the 2024 deep-linear theory of chapter 5 pushed further.
