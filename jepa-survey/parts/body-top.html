Every model on this page plays the same game. It gets to see part of something, it must guess the hidden part, and it makes that guess with a predictor that works in a space of meaning rather than in pixels. The miss is what it learns from. One color key holds for the whole page:

context: what the model sees target: what it must predict the predictor and its guess loss, energy, gradient frozen or discarded: the target encoder, pixels thrown away

Each interactive carries a badge. Computed means a real formula or configuration from a paper, run directly or on a small toy. Toy model means an illustration of the mechanism, not the paper's numbers. Sourced means what is drawn comes from the papers or from a dated arXiv query. The three-dimensional figures can be turned by dragging (or with the arrow keys once focused). In an equation, hover or tap a term that names part of a figure: that part lights up.
