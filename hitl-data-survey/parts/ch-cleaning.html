Which labels to re-check first

Re-checking labels is active learning again, applied to images that already have a label. The reviewer's time is the budget, and the order of the queue decides how much of it is wasted.

The naive order is random, and it fixes errors at the rate they occur: 9.9% of the flipped labels after 5,000 reviews. Sorting by confident learning's normalized margin fixes 25.3% with the same effort.

Bernhardt et al. (2022) add a second idea. Some images are not mislabeled but genuinely ambiguous; a reviewer looks, hesitates, and the image needs several more votes before a majority forms. Their score puts clear errors first and ambiguous images last:

\htmlData{step=1, link=phi}{\Phi(\mathbf{x}, \hat{\mathbf{l}}; \theta)} = \htmlData{step=2, link=phi}{\mathrm{CE}(\hat{\mathbf{l}}, p_\theta)} - \htmlData{step=3, link=phi}{\mathrm{H}(p_\theta)}

Bernhardt et al. (2022), Eq. 2. \mathrm{CE}: cross-entropy of the current label counts \hat{\mathbf{l}} against the model's prediction (high when the label looks wrong). \mathrm{H}: entropy of the prediction (high when the image is ambiguous). This is the ambiguity term that BALD also subtracts (chapter 3); both scores push ambiguous images down the queue.

In their simulation, with several noisy annotators per image, even the plainly trained (vanilla) selector reached 90% correct labels with 2.5x fewer re-annotations than random on CIFAR-10H and 3x fewer on a chest X-ray benchmark. In our one-look simulation the entropy term cannot help, and \Phi fixes 23.3% after 5,000 reviews, close to the margin order. Their recommendation on where to spend the budget matches chapter 8: "we generally recommend prioritising the cleaning of evaluation data up to an acceptable level before applying any remaining budget to clean the training set".

The labeling decision

Spend review time on the evaluation set first. Then order the training-set queue by how wrong the label looks (lowest margin, or highest Phi when ambiguous images need several votes), and stop when the queue's hit rate drops toward the base error rate.
