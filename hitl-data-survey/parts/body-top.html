Labels in the loop

A vision model learns from the labels people give it. Every round of that loop makes two decisions: which images get a label, and which labels to believe. This page builds both from scratch.

The page uses one color key throughout. Blue is the model: its predictions, its probabilities, the images it picks. Yellow is the human: labels, label requests, review. Green is a label we keep, red is a wrong label or a loss, and brown is the unlabeled pool, including images picked at random from it.

Sourced numbers means the published numbers of the paper named in the caption. Image tiles are real CIFAR-10 training images, drawn at their true 32 by 32 pixels.
