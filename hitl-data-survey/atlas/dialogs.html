CIFAR-10

60,000 small color images (32 by 32 pixels) in ten classes: airplane, automobile, bird, cat, deer, dog, frog, horse, ship, truck. 50,000 are for training and 10,000 for testing. Most papers on this tour use it, often with labels flipped on purpose to study noise. The images on this stage are real CIFAR-10 training images, drawn at their true size.

Pool-based active learning

Hold a small labeled set and a large unlabeled pool. Each round: train a model, score every pool image, send the top few to a person (the "oracle"), add the new labels, repeat until the label budget is spent. The whole method is the score.

Probability vector

A classifier's last layer outputs one number per class; softmax turns them into probabilities that sum to one. With three classes the vector is a point in a triangle: a corner means "certainly this class", the center means "no idea". These probabilities are often over-confident, so they measure uncertainty with a bent ruler.

Entropy

H(p) = -sum_i p_i log p_i. It is zero when one class has all the probability and largest when all classes are equally likely (log of the number of classes). It counts small probabilities too, unlike margin, which looks only at the top two classes.

MC dropout

Dropout switches off a random set of units in every forward pass. Normally it is used only in training. Keep it on at test time and each pass is a slightly different network; Gal and Ghahramani (arXiv 2015, ICML 2016) showed this approximates sampling networks from a Bayesian posterior. T passes give T predictions for one image.

BALD

Bayesian Active Learning by Disagreement: the entropy of the average prediction minus the average entropy of the individual predictions. It is the mutual information between the label and the model's weights: how much the label would tell you about which network is right. High only when the passes are individually confident but disagree.

k-Center

Choose b new centers so that the largest distance from any pool point to its nearest center (labeled or new) is as small as possible. Exact solutions are NP-hard; the greedy rule (repeatedly add the point farthest from all centers) is within a factor of 2 of the best radius.

Typicality

TypiClust's score: the inverse of the mean distance from an image to its K = 20 nearest neighbours in a self-supervised embedding. High typicality means a dense region. TypiClust clusters the pool and labels the most typical image of each cluster that has no label yet.

Confident joint

A count matrix: rows are given labels, columns are the class a held-out model confidently predicts. An image counts in column j only if the model's probability for j is at least the per-class threshold t_j, the average probability the model gives class j on images labeled j. Off-diagonal cells are suspects; their share of the total estimates the error rate.
