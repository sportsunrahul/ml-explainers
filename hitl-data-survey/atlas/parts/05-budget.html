Why random is hard to beat

Budgets, baselines and bias

Three reasons. A well-regularized model gains more than clever selection does: in Munjal et al., augmentation plus weight averaging lifted random sampling from 69.16% to 82.16% at 10% of CIFAR-10, and in a transfer test the active-learning gains over random shrank to under two points. At tiny budgets the right pick is the opposite of uncertain: label images (TypiClust). And an actively chosen set is biased, so it cannot be your test set.
