Co-teaching and DivideMix

Train only on the small-loss part of each batch, and let two networks pick for each other so neither trains on its own mistakes. In our run, the kept set was 92.9% clean at epoch 10 and averaged 78.56% test accuracy over the last ten epochs, against 60.58% for plain training.

Computed: one real batch of 32 images sorted by each network's loss at epoch 10; the kept images are outlined, flipped labels carry a red strike.

Fit two bumps to the loss histogram. Images in the low bump keep their label; images in the high bump lose the label but keep the image, and semi-supervised learning guesses a label for them. DivideMix reached 94.6% on CIFAR-10 with 50% symmetric noise (best epoch).
