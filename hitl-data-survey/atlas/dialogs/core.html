CIFAR-10

60,000 small color images (32 by 32 pixels) in ten classes: airplane, automobile, bird, cat, deer, dog, frog, horse, ship, truck. 50,000 are for training and 10,000 for testing. Most papers on this tour use it, often with labels flipped on purpose to study noise. The images on this stage are real CIFAR-10 training images, drawn at their true size.

Pool-based active learning

Hold a small labeled set and a large unlabeled pool. Each round: train a model, score every pool image, send the top few to a person (the "oracle"), add the new labels, repeat until the label budget is spent. The whole method is the score.

Probability vector

A classifier's last layer outputs one number per class; softmax turns them into probabilities that sum to one. With three classes the vector is a point in a triangle: a corner means "certainly this class", the center means "no idea". These probabilities are often over-confident, so they measure uncertainty with a bent ruler.

Entropy

H(p) = -sum_i p_i log p_i. It is zero when one class has all the probability and largest when all classes are equally likely (log of the number of classes). It counts small probabilities too, unlike margin, which looks only at the top two classes.
