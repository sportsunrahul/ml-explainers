SAM on a budget

SAM's split into a heavy encoder and a light decoder has a direct consequence: to make SAM fast, you only have to replace the encoder. Guess first: how much of the time per image is the encoder?

MobileSAM (June 2023) measured SAM's ViT-H encoder at ~452ms per image on one GPU, against 4ms for the mask decoder. Its fix is decoupled distillation: train a small encoder, TinyViT with 5.78M parameters against ViT-H's 632M (as MobileSAM counts the encoder; SAM's own Fig. 13 gives 636M for its ViT-H model), to reproduce ViT-H's image embedding with a plain MSE loss. No prompts and no masks are needed for this step, so it ran on a single GPU in less than one day, on 1% of SA-1B. SAM's own decoder then works with the new encoder unchanged. The new encoder takes ~8ms. One caution: MobileSAM scores itself by agreement with SAM's masks (mIoU 0.7447), not against human labels.

EfficientSAM (December 2023) pretrains the small encoder first: it hides 75% of the image patches and learns to reconstruct SAM's features for them (SAMI, a masked-image pretraining as in MAE), then fine-tunes on SA-1B. On COCO, with boxes from a ViTDet detector as prompts, EfficientSAM-S reaches 44.4 AP against SAM's 46.5 and MobileSAM's 38.7, in EfficientSAM's own measurement.

EfficientViT-SAM (February 2024) redesigns the encoder and measures everything on one setup: single NVIDIA A100 GPU with TensorRT, fp16. SAM-ViT-H runs 11 images per second; EfficientViT-SAM-L2 runs 538 at a COCO mAP of 46.6 against SAM's 46.5. The paper calls this a 48.9x speedup without accuracy loss. That holds on COCO; on LVIS, L2 scores 42.7 against SAM's 44.2.

For video the move is not enough. EdgeTAM (January 2025) found that a small image encoder alone does not make SAM 2 fast, because memory attention dominates the time, so it shrinks memory attention for phones: 15.7 frames per second on an iPhone 15 Pro Max against 0.7 for SAM 2-B+ and SAM 2.1-B+, at a cost in accuracy (SA-V val J&F 72.3 against 73.6 for SAM 2-B+ and 76.8 for SAM 2.1-B+). EfficientSAM3 (November 2025) proposes to distil SAM 3 into nine small students, in stages that train on SA-1B, SA-V and SAM 3's data, but its first version has no results yet. MobileSAMv2 speeds up a different part: SAM's automatic "segment everything" mode, where most time goes to decoding a grid of points. A small detector proposes boxes instead, which cuts prompt sampling plus decoding from 6464ms to 97ms; decoding alone goes from 6400ms to 50ms.

Going the other way: HQ-SAM

SAM's masks miss thin structures and fine boundaries; its own paper says so. HQ-SAM (June 2023) keeps SAM frozen and adds a single learned output token plus a fusion of early and late encoder features, about 5.1M trainable parameters on top of SAM's ViT-L model, trained in 4 hours on 8 RTX 3090 on 44K fine-grained masks. On the DIS benchmark of detailed objects, the boundary metric mBIoU goes from 52.8 to 70.4, at 4.8 against 5.0 frames per second (ViT-L). For inspection, where defects are often thin, this is the variant to know (our reading).
