Calibration

Choosing the clip value for an activation tensor

Calibrator

Activations depend on the input image, so their range comes from data. runs a few hundred representative images through the float network, records a histogram per tensor, and picks one clip value.

Max keeps every value, so one outlier sets the spacing for all. A clips a fixed share of the tail. Entropy picks the clip whose quantized histogram differs least from the original by . MSE picks the clip with the smallest squared error.

Guess before you press "entropy (KL)": will it clip more values than the percentile, or fewer? Here it clips fewer, because it looks at the tail's shape rather than a fixed count.

Clipping a few outliers buys finer marks for everything else. Try more than one calibrator and measure on your own validation set.
