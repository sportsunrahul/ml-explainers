# han2015 -- Learning both Weights and Connections for Efficient Neural Networks (BRANCH)

## Identity
- Title: Learning both Weights and Connections for Efficient Neural Networks
- arXiv 1506.02626, read v3 (2015-10-30). v1 submitted 2015-06-08 (abs.html).
- Authors: Song Han ... William J. Dally (Stanford/NVIDIA). Venue: NIPS 2015 (abs.html comments).

## Key idea (two sentences)
Train, prune every connection whose weight magnitude is below a threshold, then retrain the surviving weights
(keeping their trained values, not re-initializing), and iterate. This cuts parameters 9x (AlexNet) and 13x
(VGG-16) with no accuracy loss.

## Defining rule
- Threshold: "a quality parameter multiplied by the standard deviation of a layer's weights" (Sec. 4).
  No equation for it in the paper.
- Dropout during retraining (Eq. 2, label `eq_dropout`): `D_r = D_o \sqrt{\frac{C_{ir}}{C_{io}}}`, with
  `C_i = N_i N_{i-1}` (Eq. 1). D_o / D_r = original / retraining dropout rate; C_io / C_ir = connections in
  layer i before / after pruning.
- Retraining lr: 1/10 of original (LeNets), 1/100 (AlexNet). CONV and FC are retrained separately
  (fix one while retraining the other) (Sec. 3.3, 4).
- Iterative pruning raises AlexNet from 5x (single aggressive step) to 9x (Sec. 3.4). VGG-16: five iterations.

## Key numbers (Table 1; errors top-1 / top-5 %)
| network | ref err | pruned err | params | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LeNet-300-100 | 1.64 | 1.59 | 267K -> 22K | 12x |
| LeNet-5 | 0.80 | 0.77 | 431K -> 36K | 12x |
| AlexNet | 42.78 / 19.73 | 42.77 / 19.67 | 61M -> 6.7M | 9x |
| VGG-16 | 31.50 / 11.32 | 31.34 / 10.88 | 138M -> 10.3M | 13x |
- FLOPs: AlexNet 1.5B total, 30% remain ("computation by 3x", Table 4); VGG-16 30.9B, 21% remain ("by 5x", Table 5).
  These are FLOP counts, not measured time.
- Source inconsistency: Table 5 caption says VGG-16 weights reduced "by 12x"; Table 1 and abstract say 13x.
- AlexNet took 75 hours to train on Titan X and 173 hours to retrain after pruning (Sec. 4.2).
- Hardware: experiments on NVIDIA TitanX and GTX980 (Sec. 4).

## Outline feedback
- This is the source of the magnitude criterion for ch-prune (Sec. 4 threshold rule), not Deep Compression.
- "Learning both" speedup claims are FLOP reductions only; no wall-clock timing here.
